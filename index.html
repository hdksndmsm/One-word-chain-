    HOW IT WORKS

    1. 01There is one live word, or a short message, shared by everyone on the internet.
    2. 02Tap the pencil, write your own, pick a style and press CREATE.
    3. 03It replaces the live content for every visitor. After 10 seconds, someone else can change it.

    FAQ

    What is ONE WORD?

    A tiny shared internet experiment. At any moment there is exactly one live word or message, and anyone can replace it.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no login, no profile and no tracking of who you are. Your name is optional.

    How long can a word or message be?

    A word is 2 to 20 letters or numbers with no spaces. A message can be up to 20 words and 140 characters.

    Can I change it as often as I want?

    Yes, with a 10 second cooldown after each change. The cooldown is enforced on the server.
